Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Speak Estonian ("the App") is a language learning application. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your learning progress (completed lessons, scores, streaks, and vocabulary data) is stored locally on your device only.

Internet Usage

The App works fully offline. All audio files are bundled with the app. No internet connection is required for any functionality.

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics services, tracking tools, cookies, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage, behavior, or location.

Advertising

The App contains no advertisements.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services. All audio is bundled locally and no network requests are made during use.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and no personal data is transmitted, there is no risk of data breach from our end.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: speaklanguages@proton.me
